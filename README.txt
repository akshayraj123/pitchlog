PitchLog APK build (no Android Studio needed)
1. Create a free account at github.com and make a new repository (private is fine).
2. Upload ALL files from this folder, including the hidden .github folder, to the repository.
3. Open the Actions tab, wait about 5 minutes for "Build APK" to finish.
4. Open the finished run, download "PitchLog-apk" at the bottom, unzip it to get app-debug.apk.
5. Copy the APK to your Android phone, open it, and allow "Install unknown apps" when asked.
